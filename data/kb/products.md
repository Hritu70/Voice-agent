# NimbusDesk Plans

NimbusDesk is a cloud customer-support platform with ticketing, live chat, and an AI reply assistant.

Starter plan: $19 per agent per month. Includes email ticketing and live chat, up to 5 agents.

Growth plan: $49 per agent per month. Adds automation rules, AI reply assistant, and analytics dashboards. No agent limit.

Enterprise plan: custom pricing. Adds SSO, audit logs, a dedicated success manager, and a 99.95% uptime SLA.
